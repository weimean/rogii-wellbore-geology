## Spatial formation features (proven small gain) — 2026-07-15
- **Finding:** TVT is DETERMINISTIC in the 6 structural surfaces (ANCC..BUDA): D=TVT+Z ~ formations has per-well R²=1.0000 (100% of wells); ORACLE surfaces → eval RMSE 0.01. The old "oracle tops +0.014" probe used SCALAR per-well tops; the per-ROW structural surfaces (which encode lateral dip) are the real signal.
- **But structurally rare:** only 4.6% of eval rows have a usable OTHER-well neighbor (median nn-dist 356 ft; 96% of train wells are geographically isolated). On the ~4% close-neighbor rows: ffill 16.0 → spatial 10.5 (+5.6). Pooled vs ffill: +0.17.
- **Incremental over the rich stack (LGB+Cat, w=0.75): 13.2007 → 13.1514 = +0.0494.** Real, above noise, orthogonal to GR-matching. Features: sp_drift (boundary-anchored spatial TVT pred − ffill), sp_nndist (log nearest other-well dist = trust gate), sp_impd (imputed surface drift). Built LOWO (impute from OTHER wells) → CV-honest; `_cache_spatial.py`, `tools/spatial_formation_probe.py`, `tools/spatial_stack_test.py`.
- **SAFE (unlike geology):** GBM keys on sp_nndist → falls back to base prediction when no close neighbor. Worst case on a geographically-held-out test set = +0.00 (no harm); best case = +0.05. NOT productionized yet — pending decision (public value uncertain: depends on whether scored test wells have close TRAIN neighbors).
